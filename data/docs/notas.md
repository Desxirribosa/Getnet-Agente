# Getnet — product notes
Sources: site.getnet.com.br manuals (Get Smart, Get Clássica, Get Mini, Crediário), santander.com.br/getnet

## Get Clássica vs Get Smart
Get Clássica: keypad terminal with a display, 3G chip with free data plan + Wi-Fi + Bluetooth,
contactless (NFC), prints receipts on thermal paper, battery 4-8 hours. Lower entry price.
Get Smart: Android smart POS with touchscreen, 4G + Wi-Fi + Bluetooth, camera, apps, Kiosk Mode.
Settings menu ("Configurar") includes a connection test and a hardware test. Allows requesting
receivables advance (antecipação) directly on the terminal. Accepts Pix by QR Code or NFC.

## Pix on the card machine
To receive Pix on a Getnet card machine (Get Mini, Get Clássica) the merchant needs a Santander
bank domicile, meaning the settlement account must be at Santander. The domicile is registered or
changed in the Getnet Brasil app. Pix must be enabled in the app before the first Pix sale.
The customer pays by scanning the QR Code shown on the machine.

## Crediário
Payment option where the end customer pays in up to 48 installments, with simulation and
contracting done on the card machine. The merchant receives the full amount within up to
2 business days after the sale, in a single payment, regardless of the number of installments.
Enable it in the Getnet Brasil app > Serviços > Crediário > Habilitar Crediário.
Regular credit card installments (parcelado) go up to 12x.

## Receivables advance (antecipação de recebíveis)
Lets the merchant receive credit card sales earlier (for example installment sales), paying a
discount fee. Can be requested in the Getnet Brasil app, on the card machine, in Santander
Internet Banking / App Santander Empresas, or with the relationship manager.
On the Get Smart: Pagamento > Antecipação > merchant password > choose card brand >
simulate > Antecipar. Always simulate first to see the net amount and fee.

## Payment link (Link Get Pay) and WhatsApp
Create a payment link in the Getnet Brasil app (Link Get Pay > Criar Link Get Pay), choose amount
and payment method, and share it via WhatsApp, Instagram, e-mail or SMS. The customer pays by card
or Pix on a secure page. No card machine needed. Getnet is also a payment partner of
WhatsApp Business, so customers can pay without leaving the chat.

## Settlement (when sales are deposited)
Money is deposited into the bank domicile account according to the plan: debit usually next
business day (D+1); credit within up to 2 business days on anticipated plans, or 30 days on
standard plans; Pix immediately for Santander-domiciled merchants. Deposits only happen on business days.

## Connection troubleshooting
1. Check the signal icons (chip, Wi-Fi). 2. Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi menu > search networks > select > password.
3. If Wi-Fi fails, use the data chip. 4. Get Smart: Configurar > Conexões > Teste de Conexão.
5. Restart the machine and charge it with the original charger.
6. If it persists: Central de Relacionamento 4002 4000 (capitals) / 4003 4000 (other regions),
WhatsApp 11 97694-6815, or request maintenance in the Getnet Brasil app.

## Transaction declined
Usually the card issuer refused the payment, not the machine (insufficient funds, wrong PIN,
expired or blocked card). Ask for another card or Pix. If every card is declined, the machine may
have a connection or configuration problem: run a R$ 0,01 test sale and contact Getnet.
